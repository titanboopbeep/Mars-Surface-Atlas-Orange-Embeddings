MARS SURFACE ATLAS — TEAM QUICK-START GUIDE
============================================

Hey team, here is the step-by-step guide on how to use our Mars terrain classifier website.
Because the website gives each person their own fresh session, STEP 1 is always training the model.


WHAT DOES THIS APP DO?
----------------------
The app analyzes Mars surface photos and sorts them into two categories:
- ROCKY: Hazardous terrain with boulders, rocks, and rough outcrops that could trap or damage a rover.
- SMOOTH: Safe terrain with flat regolith, sand, or gentle dunes that are safe for rover travel.


WORKFLOW (STEP-BY-STEP)
-----------------------

STEP 1: TRAIN THE MODEL (Start here)
Every new visitor must complete this first so the website has an active model to work with:
1. Open the website link. You will land on "01 · Train Model".
2. In the left box, upload your labelled Rocky training photos (at least 3 photos).
3. In the right box, upload your labelled Smooth training photos (at least 3 photos).
4. Click the red button: "Extract SqueezeNet Features and Train Classifier".
5. Wait a few seconds while it extracts visual features and picks the best model.
6. Once it succeeds:
   - You will see the accuracy, precision, and recall scores.
   - Any duplicate or conflicting photos will be flagged automatically.
   - Optional: Click "Download Trained Model (.joblib)" to save a copy to your computer.


STEP 2: CLASSIFY NEW MARS IMAGES
Now that your model is active:
1. Click on the "02 · Classify Images" tab at the top.
2. Drag and drop any new Mars JPG or PNG images into the upload box.
3. Click the red button: "Classify Mars Images".
4. Review the results:
   - Each photo shows whether it is ROCKY or SMOOTH.
   - Each photo displays a confidence percentage (e.g., 94% confident).
   - If an image is tricky, you will see a "Low Confidence" warning tag.
5. Click "Download Predictions CSV" to export an Excel-ready spreadsheet of all predictions.


SHORTCUT FOR NEXT TIME (Skip Step 1 with a Saved Model)
-------------------------------------------------------
If you already saved a `.joblib` file from Step 1 (or if someone on the team sends you one):
1. Go directly to "02 · Classify Images".
2. Click "Load an existing .joblib model bundle (Optional)".
3. Upload the `.joblib` file.
4. You can skip Step 1 entirely and start classifying photos immediately.


HELPFUL TIPS
------------
- Photos: Clear, well-lit surface photos give the best results.
- Duplicate Protection: The app automatically detects duplicate images to prevent biased scores.
- Low Confidence Tags: Any photo with mixed sand and pebbles will be flagged for manual review.
