MARS SURFACE ATLAS — TEAM QUICK-START GUIDE
============================================

Hey team, here is a quick guide on how to use our Mars terrain classifier website.
You do not need any coding knowledge or extra software installed. Everything runs directly in your browser.


WHAT DOES THIS APP DO?
----------------------
The app analyzes Mars surface photos and sorts them into two categories:
- ROCKY: Hazardous terrain with boulders, rocks, and rough outcrops that could trap or damage a rover.
- SMOOTH: Safe terrain with flat regolith, sand, or gentle dunes that are safe for rover travel.


HOW TO USE THE APP
------------------

STEP 1: CLASSIFYING NEW MARS PHOTOS (Most common task)
1. Open the website and click on the "02 · Classify Images" tab at the top.
2. Drag and drop any new Mars JPG or PNG images into the upload box.
3. Click the red button: "Classify Mars Images".
4. View the results:
   - Each photo gets a card showing its label (ROCKY or SMOOTH).
   - Each photo shows a confidence percentage (e.g., 94% confident).
   - If an image is tricky or ambiguous, the app shows a "Low Confidence" warning tag.
5. Click "Download Predictions CSV" if you want to export an Excel-ready spreadsheet of all results.


STEP 2: TRAINING A NEW MODEL
1. Click on the "01 · Train Model" tab.
2. In the left box, upload your rocky training photos (upload at least 3 photos).
3. In the right box, upload your smooth training photos (upload at least 3 photos).
4. Click "Extract SqueezeNet Features and Train Classifier".
5. When finished, you will see:
   - Overall accuracy, precision, and recall scores.
   - An automatic duplicate audit (flags identical photos or accidental conflicting labels).
6. Click "Download Trained Model (.joblib)" to save the trained model file to your computer.


STEP 3: RE-USING A SAVED MODEL FILE (.joblib)
- Because free cloud servers restart when inactive, you do not need to retrain from scratch every time:
1. Go to the "02 · Classify Images" tab.
2. Click the expander called "Load an existing .joblib model bundle (Optional)".
3. Upload the .joblib file that was previously downloaded.
4. You are ready to classify new photos immediately.


HELPFUL TIPS FOR THE TEAM
-------------------------
- Use clear photos: Sharp, well-lit surface photos give the most accurate predictions.
- Pay attention to "Low Confidence" tags: Photos with mixed sand and pebbles will be flagged so you can inspect them manually.
- The app automatically ignores duplicate pictures during training so your scores stay honest.
